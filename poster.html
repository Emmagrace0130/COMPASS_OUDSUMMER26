PRISMA screening protocol — every document traced, categorized, and metadata-tagged with source, page, and topic.

Technical Approach
Layer 1
Document Database

600–850 screened documents chunked, embedded (BAAI/bge-small-en), and stored in a FAISS vector index for semantic retrieval. Every chunk carries metadata: source file, page number, and topic.

Layer 2
RAG Pipeline

Top-6 semantically similar chunks retrieved per query and injected into the LLM context. Answers are grounded in retrieved passages — not model memory. Sources returned with every response.

Layer 3 — Core Innovation
Concept Map (Neo4j)

200+ nodes · 400+ connections — medications, guidelines, populations, contraindications, TN geography, and policy instruments. Graph layer adds structured relational reasoning on top of text retrieval.

Live TN Surveillance Dashboards

COMPASS integrates real Tennessee data alongside the AI chatbot — available as interactive tabs on the live platform.

  • Rx Trends — Opioid & benzo prescribing rates, all 95 counties, 2013–2024 (TN CSMD). 51% decline in opioid Rx rate since 2013.
  • Knox Co. Deaths — 2025 medical examiner autopsy data. Fentanyl in 59% of toxicology cases.
  • TN County Map — Per-capita overdose death choropleth, 95 counties.
  • Wastewater — Opioid signal from TN municipal wastewater monitoring sites.
  • NSDUH · CDC Data — National survey estimates and CDC provisional death counts for TN.
  • Treatment Gap — SAMHSA data on OUD prevalence vs. treatment access by state.
Expected Contributions
First of its Kind

First RAG + knowledge graph system built specifically for Tennessee OUD, combining clinical, policy, and surveillance data

Rigorous Evaluation

RAG vs. RAG+Graph comparison: does the concept map actually improve answer quality and consistency?

Equity Testing

Demographic vignette test ensures answers hold quality across rural, justice-involved, and underinsured TN populations

Open Science

Full reproducibility package so other states or substance use disorders can replicate or extend the system

Broader Impacts
  • Clinicians and public health workers gain plain-language, evidence-grounded OUD answers without searching multiple databases
  • UTK Institute for Public Service partnership grounds outputs in real practitioner needs
  • Graduate researcher trained in full research arc: curation, AI design, evaluation, and scientific writing
  • Aligned to NIH HEAL Initiative Goal 2-2 and CMS Rural Health Transformation
