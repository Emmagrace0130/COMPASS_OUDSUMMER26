Critical information about OUD treatment — clinical guidelines, state policy, local surveillance data — is scattered across dozens of agencies, databases, and PDF reports. Clinicians, researchers, and policymakers can't easily access or synthesize it in time to act.

What is COMPASS

An AI Research Assistant for OUD

COMPASS is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot that puts 150+ clinical guidelines, peer-reviewed papers, and Tennessee policy documents at your fingertips. Ask questions in plain language and get evidence-based answers with citations — in seconds.

RAG-Powered AI Tennessee-Specific Evidence-Grounded Free to Access

How It Works — RAG Pipeline
1

User Asks a Question

Type any OUD-related question in plain English — clinical, policy, or data. No special syntax needed.

e.g. "What's the recommended buprenorphine dose for fentanyl-dependent patients?"
2

Vector Similarity Search

The question is encoded as a vector. FAISS searches 150+ indexed documents and retrieves the most semantically relevant passages.

FAISS · fastembed · semantic search
3

Context Passed to LLM

Retrieved passages are bundled with the question and sent to the AI model — Claude (Anthropic), Ollama (local), or HuggingFace.

Claude AI · Ollama · HuggingFace
4

Grounded Answer Returned

The model generates a cited answer based only on retrieved evidence — not hallucination. Every response links to source documents you can verify.

Citations included · Source-verified
